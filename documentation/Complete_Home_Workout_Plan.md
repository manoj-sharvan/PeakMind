# 1. PROGRAM OBJECTIVES

This program has four objectives.

| Objective | Main training focus |
|---|---|
| Erectile dysfunction | Aerobic fitness, pelvic-floor coordination and lower-body strength |
| Premature ejaculation | Pelvic-floor awareness, relaxation and start-stop practice |
| Belly/body fat | Full-body conditioning, strength and aerobic exercise |
| Body heat management | Breathing, cool-down, hydration and recovery |

**Important:** Exercise can support sexual health and physical fitness, but it is not a guaranteed cure for ED or PE. Exercise also cannot permanently lower normal internal body temperature.

# 3. BEGINNER LEVEL

**Duration:** Weeks 1–4

**Primary objective:** Learn exercise technique, improve mobility, build cardiovascular fitness and understand pelvic-floor contraction and relaxation.

## A. ED and PE exercises

### 1. Basic Male Kegel

**Purpose:** Pelvic-floor strength and coordination.

Steps:
1. Sit on a stable chair.
2. Relax your stomach and thighs.
3. Gently contract the muscles used to stop passing gas.
4. Lift gently.
5. Hold for 3 seconds.
6. Release completely for 6 seconds.

Training:
- Sets: 2
- Repetitions: 8
- Hold: 3 seconds
- Relaxation: 6 seconds
- Frequency: 3 days per week

### 2. Quick Kegels

**Purpose:** Controlled contraction and release.

Steps:
1. Sit comfortably.
2. Contract the pelvic floor for 1 second.
3. Release completely.
4. Repeat without holding your breath.

Training:
- Sets: 2
- Repetitions: 5–8
- Frequency: 2–3 days per week

### 3. Reverse Kegel Awareness

**Purpose:** Pelvic-floor relaxation.

Steps:
1. Lie down or sit comfortably.
2. Relax your abdomen.
3. Inhale slowly.
4. Allow the pelvic floor to soften.
5. Exhale without squeezing.
6. Repeat.

Training:
- Duration: 3–5 minutes
- Frequency: Daily

Do not forcefully push downwards.

### 4. Diaphragmatic Breathing

**Purpose:** Relaxation and breathing coordination.

Steps:
1. Lie on your back.
2. Bend your knees.
3. Place a hand on your stomach.
4. Inhale slowly.
5. Allow your abdomen to rise.
6. Exhale slowly.

Training:
- Duration: 5 minutes
- Frequency: Daily

# 6. EJACULATION-CONTROL PRACTICE

These are behavioural exercises, not ordinary muscle-strengthening exercises.

## 49. Start-Stop Technique

Purpose: Improve awareness of rising arousal.

Steps:
1. During private sexual activity, notice your level of arousal.
2. Stop stimulation when you approach the point just before ejaculation becomes inevitable.
3. Breathe slowly.
4. Relax your abdomen and pelvic floor.
5. Wait until arousal decreases.
6. Resume if comfortable.
7. Repeat 2–3 cycles.

Practice occasionally. There is no requirement to achieve a specific duration.

## 50. Arousal Awareness

1. Notice changes in breathing.
2. Notice pelvic-floor tension.
3. Identify when arousal increases.
4. Pause before reaching the point of no return.
5. Practise relaxing without forcefully pushing.

## 51. Relaxation Breathing

- Duration: 5 minutes
- Frequency: Daily

Use slow abdominal breathing and avoid unnecessary muscle tightening.

# 9. BODY HEAT MANAGEMENT

Feeling hot does not necessarily mean your internal body temperature is high.

## Daily recovery exercises

| Exercise | Duration |
|---|---:|
| Diaphragmatic breathing | 5 minutes |
| Child's Pose | 30 seconds |
| Cat-Cow | 2 minutes |
| Gentle hip stretching | 2 minutes |
| Slow indoor walking | 3 minutes |

## Daily habits

1. Drink water regularly according to thirst and activity.
2. Prefer morning or evening exercise.
3. Avoid exercising in excessively hot rooms.
4. Wear lightweight clothing.
5. Use a fan or ventilated room.
6. Take breaks between exercise sets.
7. Cool down gradually after workouts.
8. Avoid excessive exercise when feeling unwell.

If you have persistent heat intolerance, excessive sweating or unexplained flushing, seek medical assessment. If you have an actual fever, do not exercise until recovered.

# 12. SAFETY RULES

1. Do not train through pain.
2. Do not hold your breath during exercise.
3. Avoid excessive Kegel training.
4. Do not perform repeated urine-stopping exercises.
5. Avoid forceful reverse Kegels.
6. Avoid high-impact movements until your conditioning improves.
7. Rest if your muscles are excessively tired.
8. Avoid exercising in extreme heat.
9. Stop exercise if you experience chest pain, faintness or unusual breathlessness.
10. Seek urgent help for confusion, collapse or suspected heatstroke.

If pelvic-floor exercises cause pelvic pain, urinary symptoms or increased tension, stop the contractions and seek assessment from a pelvic-floor physiotherapist.

# 13. MEDICAL ASSESSMENT

Exercise is not a replacement for investigating persistent ED or PE.

For ED, discuss:
- Blood pressure.
- Blood glucose or HbA1c.
- Lipid profile.
- Total testosterone when clinically appropriate.
- Sleep, stress and other possible contributing factors.

For PE, discuss:
- Whether the pattern is lifelong or acquired.
- Ejaculation control and distress.
- Pelvic-floor overactivity or dysfunction.
- Appropriate behavioural and medical treatment options.

Do not start, stop or adjust prescription sexual-health medication based on workout progress.
